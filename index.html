Campus Cup

Race around the school-inspired oval, keep your kart on the track, and beat the rival racers to the finish line.

Collect the gold boost sparks along the straightaways. Grass slows you down, and bumps with other karts cost speed.

This is an unofficial fan-made game and is not affiliated with Chaminade High School.
